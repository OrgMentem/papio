Where papio opens tabs

papio opens a browser tab to fetch each paper and only brings it forward when you are needed (for example institutional sign-in). Choose where those handoff tabs live.

Publisher terms & conditions

Some publishers require accepting their terms & conditions before a download. When enabled, papio accepts these automatically on your behalf. This is a legal agreement to each publisher's terms; leave it off to accept them yourself in the browser.

Auto-accept publisher terms
Not set yet — papio asks on your first download.

Firefox data collection

Firefox requires your permission before older versions can send diagnostic page captures to the local papio app. Captures are sanitized before they leave the extension.

Keep your institution session warm

papio keeps your library session fresh by refreshing a pinned background tab. Turn this off if you prefer to sign in manually for each handoff.

Your library

These resolver hosts come from your papio configuration and aren't covered by the generic Ex Libris hosts below. Grant access so papio can follow your library's "full text" link automatically instead of leaving it for you to click.

    Library resolver access

    Firefox asks you to grant this access at runtime; Chrome grants it when the extension is installed. papio uses it to resolve your library's links when completing an explicitly requested download.

      All-sites access

      This is the broadest permission: it lets papio reach any HTTPS site, including unlisted providers. It covers every provider below; turn it off to manage sources individually.

        Page scanning

        Page scans are local, run only when you invoke them, read the top frame only, and are separate from provider download access below. Granting a publisher permission does not let papio scan that site until you allow scanning here or on first use.

        No sites are allowed for page scanning.

          Provider access

          Grant access per source. Access is only used to complete one explicitly requested download after you have signed in yourself. Login, MFA, and CAPTCHAs always stay with you. Revoking returns the source to assisted behaviour immediately. Revoke all provider access also removes all-sites access when it is enabled.
